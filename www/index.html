About ClassConnect

ClassConnect

Connecting college students, one class at a time.

ClassConnect was built by Hanz Dee Dalmino, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student in Cebu Technological University - Main Campus. It is a comprehensive student management platform designed to help college students stay organized, collaborate with classmates, track General Weighted Average (GWA), and store official curriculum PDF syllabi all in one place.

Privacy Policy & Terms

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

ClassConnect respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our platform.

Information We Collect

  • Name, email address, and section details
  • Academic information (subjects, grades, assignments)
  • Profile photo and bio (optional)

Data Storage

All your data — including your profile, subjects, assignments, grades, schedule, curriculum, and posts — is stored securely in Supabase, a cloud-hosted PostgreSQL database. Supabase enforces Row-Level Security (RLS) policies on every table, which means your authentication token is required to access your records. No other user can read or modify your data. The only item stored locally on your device is your font preference setting (saved in localStorage).

Terms of Service

By using ClassConnect, you agree to these Terms of Service. Always back up your academic information.
