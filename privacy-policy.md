# Privacy Policy for ClassConnect

**Last Updated:** 2026

ClassConnect respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

## Data We Collect
- Name, email address, and section details
- Academic information (subjects, grades, assignments)
- Profile photo and bio (optional)

## How We Use Your Data
- To provide the ClassConnect service
- To connect you with classmates
- To store your academic information

## Data Storage
All your data is stored securely in Supabase, a cloud-hosted PostgreSQL database with Row-Level Security (RLS). Only your authenticated account can access your data.

## Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties.
